# Privacy Policy (DRAFT)

> **STATUS: DRAFT — NOT YET IN FORCE.** This document was prepared as a working draft to be finalised and approved by a qualified Nigerian lawyer and the organisation's data-protection contact before publication or reliance. It is not legal advice.

KnowYourRights helps people in Nigeria understand their rights. We collect as
little as possible and never sell data. This policy is written against the Nigeria
Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

## Who we are
KnowYourRights ("we"). Data-protection contact: **[DP CONTACT — TO BE APPOINTED]**.

## Using the app without an account
Core rights, emergency scenarios, and the directory work with **no account and no
personal data**. You can read your rights anonymously and offline.

## What we process, and the lawful basis
| Data | Why | Lawful basis (NDPA) |
|------|-----|---------------------|
| Email + password (accounts) | Sign-in for safety features and contributions | Contract |
| Profile name & phone (encrypted) | Identify you in a Custody Alert | Consent |
| Emergency contacts (encrypted) | To alert them on your behalf | Consent (yours; you attest theirs) |
| Custody Alert location | Sent to your chosen contacts during an incident | Consent / vital interests |
| AI question telemetry (no question text) | Cost and reliability monitoring | Legitimate interest |

We **do not** retain the text of AI questions, and Incognito Ask records nothing.

## Retention
Each class is kept only as long as `RETENTION-SCHEDULE.md` allows and is then
deleted automatically. Location is purged as soon as the incident window closes.

## Your rights (NDPA)
Access, correction, erasure, and portability — see `DataSubjectRights.md`. In the
app: **Settings → Your data → Export / Delete**.

## Sharing
Custody Alert content goes only to the contacts you chose. We use processors
(email/SMS delivery, hosting) under contract. We never sell personal data.

## Security
TLS in transit; encryption at rest for profile, contact, and location fields;
role-based access; audit logging of privileged actions.

## Changes
Material changes will be notified in-app. Contact the DP contact for any question.
